## What it is

Sunburn is skin damage from too much sun. It usually heals in a few days.
The skin may look red, feel hot, and hurt when touched.

## What to do

1. Put aloe vera gel on the burn 2–3 times a day. It cools the skin and
   helps it feel better.
2. Drink plenty of water today and tomorrow. Sunburn pulls fluid toward
   the skin, so your body needs extra.
3. Use cool, wet cloths on sore spots for 10 minutes at a time. Do not
   put ice directly on skin.
4. Take your usual pain medicine, if your care team says it is safe for
   you. Follow the label exactly.
5. Wear loose, soft clothes while the skin heals.

## What NOT to do

- Do not go back in the sun until your skin has healed.
- Do not pop blisters. The skin over them is a natural bandage.
- Do not pick or peel sunburned skin.

## Call your clinic if

- You get a fever or chills.
- The redness spreads, leaks pus, or feels hotter and more painful after
  a few days.
- You feel dizzy or lightheaded when you stand up.
