## Post-Discharge Fever Management Protocol

In the event that your child develops an elevated temperature following
discharge from our facility, it is of paramount importance that the
temperature be measured accurately utilizing an appropriate
thermometric device and that the resultant measurements be recorded
systematically so as to facilitate the longitudinal assessment of
febrile trends by the clinical team.

Medication administration instructions. Weight-based acetaminophen
dosing (10-15 mg/kg every 4-6 hours, not exceeding 5 doses daily) should
be administered per the parameters provided at discharge; ibuprofen
(10 mg/kg every 6-8 hours) may additionally be considered for children
over six months of age. Caregivers are advised that aspirin should be
strictly avoided in pediatric patients due to the association with
Reye's syndrome.

Families should be counseled that the maintenance of hydration status is
essential during febrile illness, with smaller, more frequent offerings
of fluids being preferable to larger volume boluses in the infant and
toddler population. Cotton clothing consistent with ambient temperature
is recommended, as both overbundling and aggressive cooling measures may
be counterproductive to the thermoregulatory process.

Return precautions include, without limitation, seizure activity,
lethargy inconsistent with typical febrile presentations, petechial or
purpuric rash, respiratory compromise, and the persistence of fever
beyond the temporal parameters discussed during the discharge
conversation.
