# Welcome: What This Manual Is (and Is Not)

> **DISCLAIMER — EDUCATION ONLY, NEVER MEDICAL ADVICE.** This manual teaches
> healthcare workers how to draft plain-language patient-education handouts
> with AI assistance. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for your
> facility's review and approval process. See [00-disclaimer.md](00-disclaimer.md).
> **Never put patient data into any AI tool.**

## Who this is for

You are a nurse, tech, therapist, educator, or unit-based champion who has been
asked (or has volunteered) to make patient handouts people can actually read.
You have heard that AI can "write things for you," and you want to use it
safely, without breaking policy, without leaking PHI, and without letting a
confident-sounding error reach a patient's kitchen counter.

Written the way Dylan Asdale writes: plain English, step by step, never
condescending. If a term is jargon — *teach-back*, *Flesch-Kincaid*, *hallucination* —
we define it the first time. See [10-glossary.md](10-glossary.md).

## What this manual is NOT

- **Not medical advice.** Nothing here tells a patient what to do about a
  condition, and nothing here tells you how to treat anyone.
- **Not a clinical reference.** Verify every clinical fact against approved
  sources, never against the AI itself.
- **Not cloud-first.** Every workflow here assumes the safest option: no patient
  data anywhere, no keys or accounts needed to follow the exercises, and tools
  that can run locally or on paper when the network is not an option.
- **Not a policy loophole.** Your facility's AI, privacy, and education policies
  always outrank anything printed here.

## The five rules, in one breath

1. Education only. Never medical advice.
2. No patient data in any tool, ever.
3. AI drafts; a qualified human verifies every claim; your facility approves.
4. Aim for grade 6–8 reading level, and prove it with a readability check.
5. Teach back, don't hand out: confirm the patient understood the key points.

Those five rules appear again and again. When a chapter gets complicated, come
back to this list and the one-page [09-prompt-library.md](09-prompt-library.md).
