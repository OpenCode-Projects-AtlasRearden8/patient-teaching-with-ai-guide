# Education Only: The Disclaimer We Mean It

> **DISCLAIMER — EDUCATION ONLY, NEVER MEDICAL ADVICE.**
> This manual is educational material for healthcare workers learning to use
> AI writing tools. It is **not medical advice**, **not clinical guidance**,
> and **not a substitute for your facility's policies, your scope of practice,
> or a licensed clinician's judgment**. Every handout you draft with AI must be
> reviewed and approved through your facility's normal review process before it
> is printed, posted, emailed, or handed to a patient. If you are ever unsure,
> stop and ask your educator, supervisor, or clinical librarian.

This document is the "always-on" disclaimer. It is repeated in shortened form
throughout the manual because it matters:

- **AI drafts; humans approve.** Nothing an AI writes should reach paper without
  a qualified human checking every clinical claim.
- **No patient data, ever.** Never paste patient names, chart details, dates of
  birth, MRNs, or any protected health information (PHI) into an AI tool.
- **Follow your facility.** If your workplace prohibits AI tools for this
  purpose, that rule wins. This manual does not override any policy.
- **Education is the product.** What you get from this manual is skill and
  judgment, not a shortcut around review.

## How to cite the spirit of this manual

When you share your handouts internally, keep a line like this in the footer:

```text
Drafted with AI assistance; reviewed and approved by [Name/Credentials], [Date].
Not clinical advice. Follow facility policy.
```

## If in doubt

If you cannot confirm something the AI wrote against a trusted source —
your facility's patient-education library, UpToDate, a national association,
or a specialist colleague — it does not go to paper. There is no deadline so
urgent that it justifies an unverified dose, sign, or instruction line.
