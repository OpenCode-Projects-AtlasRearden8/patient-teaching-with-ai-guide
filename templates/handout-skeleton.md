# Handout Skeleton (fill in, then draft)

> **DISCLAIMER — EDUCATION ONLY, NEVER MEDICAL ADVICE.** This skeleton is a
> structure for drafting patient-education materials; the facts you write into
> it must come from you and your approved sources, never invented, and never
> from a specific patient's chart. **Never put patient data into any AI tool.**
> Workflow: [docs/02-drafting-handouts.md](../docs/02-drafting-handouts.md).

Copy this whole skeleton into a new draft, fill only the brackets from your
Define step (your own words, your approved facts), then hand the result to
your AI tool with the Master Draft Prompt
([docs/09-prompt-library.md](../docs/09-prompt-library.md)) — or rewrite it yourself
by hand. The skeleton teaches the AI the shape so it spends its effort on
wording, not on inventing content.

```text
CARING FOR [CONDITION] AT HOME

What it is:
  [2 plain sentences — what it is, how long it usually lasts]

What to do:
  1. [one action, starts with a verb]
  2. [one action, starts with a verb]
  3. [one action, starts with a verb]
  (3-6 actions total; more than 6 means the sheet is trying to do too much)

What NOT to do:
  - [concrete avoidance; no hedging]

Call your clinic if:
  - [specific symptom a patient can see or feel]
  - [specific symptom a patient can see or feel]
  - Phone: [call number from your facility]

Go now (emergency) if:
  - [severe symptom with an immediate action]
  - Emergency number: [from your facility]

This sheet is for education only. It does not replace advice from
your care team.

Source: [facility handout # / approved source]
Drafted with AI assistance; human-reviewed before approval.
Approved by: ____  Date: ____  Version: ____
```

Rules for filling it in:

- **Every blank is yours.** If you cannot fill one from an approved source,
  write "ASK YOUR CARE TEAM" in that spot — never a number from memory of a
  specific patient, never a number the AI offered.
- **The "go now" block stays** even when the list is short. It is the most-used
  block on the page.
- **One page, or shorter.** If content cannot fit, split into a second sheet —
  do not shrink the font.
