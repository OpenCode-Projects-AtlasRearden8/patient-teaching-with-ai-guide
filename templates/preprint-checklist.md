# Pre-Print Checklist (print this card)

> **DISCLAIMER — EDUCATION ONLY, NEVER MEDICAL ADVICE.** This checklist is a
> workflow aid for healthcare workers; it never replaces facility policy,
> clinical review, or your scope of practice. **Never put patient data into
> any AI tool.** Full version: [docs/06-facility-policy-checklist.md](../docs/06-facility-policy-checklist.md).

Print, laminate, keep at the drafting workstation. Nine checks, one look:

- [ ] **1. TOOL** — AI tool name/version: ____________ Cleared by facility
      policy. Processing location known (local / enterprise / public).
- [ ] **2. NO PATIENT DATA** — nothing from any real patient's record,
      conversation, or photo entered the draft, the tool, or its transcripts.
- [ ] **3. SOURCE OF TRUTH** — every clinical fact traced to
      (source/number): ____________
- [ ] **4. REVIEW** — reviewed by (name, credentials): ____________ on
      date: ____________ Second reviewer (new topics): ____________
- [ ] **5. READABILITY** — Flesch-Kincaid grade: ______ (target 6–8,
      pass bar 8.0). Output saved at: ____________
- [ ] **6. TEACH-BACK** — at least one teach-back question per key action
      step. (Pattern: [docs/04-teach-back.md](../docs/04-teach-back.md).)
- [ ] **7. APPROVAL** — approved through (committee/educator): ____________
      on date: ____________
- [ ] **8. FOOTER** — education-only line, source line, approval name/date,
      and "Drafted with AI assistance; human-reviewed" line present.
- [ ] **9. VERSION** — file numbered/dated; obsolete copies archived;
      printed stock replaced.

**The 60-second stop check** ([docs/05-spotting-ai-errors.md](../docs/05-spotting-ai-errors.md)):

1. Can I point to a source for every fact?
2. Would I be comfortable if this exact sheet went home with a family member?
3. Does the "call now" block exist, is it specific, and is the number right?

If any answer is no — the sheet stops. Not ships.
